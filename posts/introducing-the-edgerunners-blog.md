# A home for what we learn

We are opening the EdgeRunners blog as a place to share product updates,
practical guides for running multiplayer game servers, and engineering notes
from behind the platform.

The first articles will be published directly as Markdown while we keep the
workflow intentionally simple. More stories are on the way.
